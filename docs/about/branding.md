# Branding

The visual identity of **afmpeg** and its downloadable brand assets. afmpeg is
a headline project with its own palette rather than the shared phpboyscout
teal and amber, so it reads as itself first and as part of the estate second.

---

## The mark

afmpeg's logo is a **play triangle standing on a short fanned stack of slabs**.
The slabs are the virtual filesystem afmpeg runs FFmpeg over: layered,
in memory, and not a disk. The play triangle is the media work that happens on
top of it. Electric blue slabs with sunflower edges, a sunflower play mark,
midnight ink linework.

Sky is electric blue lifted for contrast against ink, used for links on the
dark scheme. Midnight ink and bone are shared with
[ffmpeg-wasi](https://ffmpeg-wasi.phpboyscout.uk/about/branding/), the engine
afmpeg runs, so the two read as a pair.
